## SECTION 1.0: INSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE & TRANSPARENCY MANDATE

1.1. **The Superpower Standards Board (SSB)** operates as the internal clinical, ethical, and quality governance committee of The Superpower Project Ltd. The Board is composed of senior clinical advisors, registered social care governance professionals, safeguarding leads, and non-clinical facilitators.

1.2. **Absolute Transparency Mandate:** In strict adherence to UK consumer protection law (*Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008*) and corporate governance standards, this Recognition Scheme operates transparently as an **internal, proprietary quality distinction program**. Under no circumstances does the Board represent itself as a statutory government agency, an arm of the Care Quality Commission (CQC), or a Royal Charter body. All honors, seals, and accreditations conferred under this scheme denote verified operational compliance with The Superpower Project’s published standards of excellence.

1.3. **Purpose of Distinction:** The Scheme exists exclusively to recognize, validate, and celebrate **client care homes, regional care groups, individual care professionals, and community partner organizations** that achieve outstanding benchmarks in resident dignity, non-pharmacological reminiscence integration, and technological invisibility.

### RECOGNITION ENTRY 001: THE NATIONAL SENIOR QUALITY-OF-LIFE SEAL (NSQLS-A1)

| SPECIFICATION FIELD | DETAILS |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Accreditation Identifier** | `SSB-SEAL-2026-NSQLS-A1` |
| **Conferring Body** | The Superpower Standards Board (Clinical & Quality Directorate) |
| **Audited Entity Class** | Regulated Care Homes (Residential, Nursing, Specialist Dementia) |
| **Statutory / Clinical Benchmark** | *NICE Guideline NG97 (Section 1.4)*; *Care Act 2014 Section 1 (Wellbeing Principle)* |
| **Validation Period** | 12 Calendar Months (Subject to Annual Re-Audit) |

#### Formal Citation & Qualification Criteria
**Section 2.1.1.** The *National Senior Quality-of-Life Seal (NSQLS-A1)* is awarded to residential and nursing facilities that demonstrate exemplary commitment to person-centred narrative therapy. Qualifying homes must successfully complete a minimum of 20 facilitator-led resident biographical sessions over a 6-month period, demonstrating sustained adherence to non-pharmacological cognitive engagement standards.

**Section 2.1.2.** Assessing officers evaluate the physical environment of the partner facility, confirming that sensory stimulation protocols are tailored to fluctuating cognitive capacity, acoustic noise is regulated below clinical distress thresholds, and residents living with moderate-to-severe dementia are provided unhurried, 1-to-1 narrative validation.

**Section 2.1.3.** Receipt of the NSQLS-A1 Seal grants the care home the right to display the physical commemorative brass plaque and digital credential within their reception suites and CQC inspection portfolios, verifying that the facility actively invests in resident identity preservation and social isolation mitigation.

### RECOGNITION ENTRY 002: EXCELLENCE IN CQC SINGLE ASSESSMENT REGULATORY EVIDENCE

| SPECIFICATION FIELD | DETAILS |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Accreditation Identifier** | `SSB-AWD-2026-CQC-REG` |
| **Conferring Body** | The Superpower Standards Board (Regulatory Compliance Directorate) |
| **Audited Entity Class** | Care Home Registered Managers, Nominated Individuals & Clinical Leads |
| **Statutory / Clinical Benchmark** | *Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014: Regs 9, 10, 11, 12, 17* |
| **Validation Period** | Annual Audit Cycle |

#### Formal Citation & Qualification Criteria
**Section 2.2.1.** Conferred upon care management teams that systematically integrate structured biographical capture into their regulatory audit infrastructure. To qualify, a facility must demonstrate that life-story insights gathered through Superpower Project sessions are actively transcribed into the resident's primary Care Plan, directly informing daily care routines, dietary choices, and behavioral de-escalation strategies.

**Section 2.2.2.** Assessing auditors verify that the home maintains complete, auditable compliance folders containing:
1. Validated Mental Capacity Act 2005 consent records (Tiers 1, 2 LPA, and 3 Best-Interests determinations);
2. Quantitative resident engagement logs based on validated wellbeing affect scales;
3. Documented proof that zero hours of frontline care staff rotas were extracted to achieve these psychosocial outcomes.
