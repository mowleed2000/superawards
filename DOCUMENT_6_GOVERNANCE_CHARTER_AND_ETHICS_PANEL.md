## SECTION 1.0: CONSTITUTIONAL MANDATE & LEGAL STRUCTURE

1.1. **Corporate Entity:** The Superpower Project Ltd is a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company law (*Companies Act 2006*).

1.2. **Section 172 Duty & Stakeholder Balancing:** In fulfilling their fiduciary obligations under Section 172 of the *Companies Act 2006*, the Directors commit to:
* The long-term consequences of all organizational decisions on vulnerable elderly populations;
* The emotional, psychological, and physical safety of care home residents;
* The dignity and working conditions of frontline social care professionals;
* The highest standards of commercial transparency, environmental care, and institutional integrity.

1.3. **The Nolan Principles of Public Life:** Although a private enterprise, the Organization formally adopts the Seven Nolan Principles of Public Life across all executive operations: *Selflessness, Integrity, Objectivity, Accountability, Openness, Honesty, and Leadership*.

### 2.1. Composition of the Clinical & Ethical Advisory Panel (CEAP)
The CEAP functions as an independent consultative and oversight body. Its standing members include:
1. **Nominated Clinical Safety Officer (CSO):** A registered healthcare or senior clinical professional holding accreditation under NHS Digital Clinical Safety Standard DCB0129;
2. **Designated Caldicott Guardian:** Overseeing the lawful, ethical, and proportionate sharing of resident personal health-adjacent data;
3. **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** Ensuring ongoing registration and compliance with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under the *Data Protection Act 2018*;
4. **Lead Safeguarding Officer:** Certified in adult safeguarding (Level 3 or 4) with direct statutory liaison authority;
5. **Independent Dementia Care Advisor:** Providing guidance on non-pharmacological reminiscence methodologies and cognitive frailty.

### 2.2. Remit & Authority of the CEAP
* **Veto Power Over Technical Implementations:** The CEAP possesses absolute constitutional authority to suspend or veto any algorithmic tool, software release, or prompt framework that presents identifiable risks of sensory overload, algorithmic hallucination, or ethical breach among vulnerable adults.
* **Bi-Annual Incident Review:** The Panel convenes semi-annually to review all safeguarding incident reports, adverse reactions, whistleblowing disclosures, and equipment sanitization audit logs.
* **Annual Public Ethics Report:** The Panel publishes an annual transparency statement detailing the volume of sessions delivered, safeguarding referrals executed, and data retention sanitization audits completed.

## SECTION 3.0: CONFLICT OF INTEREST & WHISTLEBLOWING POLICY

3.1. **Strict Conflict of Interest Registry:** All directors, advisors, and staff must declare financial, personal, or commercial interests in partner care homes, software vendors, or commercial suppliers.

3.2. **Whistleblower Protections:** In full compliance with the *Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998 (PIDA 1998)*, any employee or contractor who reports safeguarding negligence, clinical non-compliance, or financial irregularity is guaranteed complete anonymity and legal protection against detrimental treatment.
